About CVApplyr

Empowering job seekers with AI-powered cover letter generation and application management.

Our Story

CVApplyr was born from a simple observation: job seekers spend countless hours crafting personalized cover letters for each application, often with mixed results. We believed there had to be a better way.

Founded in 2025 by zSellr Enterprises LLP, CVApplyr combines cutting-edge AI technology with deep understanding of recruitment processes to help job seekers create compelling, personalized cover letters in minutes, not hours.

Our Mission

Our mission is to democratize access to professional-quality job application materials. Whether you're a fresh graduate or an experienced professional, CVApplyr helps you present your best self to potential employers.

We believe that:

  • Every job seeker deserves the tools to compete effectively
  • Technology should empower, not replace, human creativity
  • The job search process should be efficient and stress-free
  • Personalization and authenticity are key to successful applications

What We Do

CVApplyr is an AI-powered platform that revolutionizes the job application process:

🤖 AI-Powered Generation

Advanced AI analyzes your resume and job details to create personalized cover letters tailored to each position.

🔍 Company Research

Automatic research on target companies helps craft letters that resonate with employers.

📧 Email Integration

Send applications directly through the platform with professional email templates.

📊 Application Tracking

Track all your applications in one place and monitor responses.

💼 Profile Management

Store resumes, photos, and signatures for quick access.

🎯 Recipient Management

Save and organize job contacts for efficient bulk applications.
