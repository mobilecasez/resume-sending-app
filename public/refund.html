Refund Policy

Last Updated: January 25, 2026

1. General Policy

At CVApplyr, we strive to provide high-quality services. This Refund Policy explains the circumstances under which refunds may be issued for credit purchases.

Important: Credits are generally non-refundable once purchased. Please read this policy carefully before making a purchase.

2. Non-Refundable Credits

Credits purchased through CVApplyr are generally non-refundable. This includes credits that have been:

  • Partially or fully used to generate cover letters
  • Used to send job applications
  • Expired due to the validity period stated at purchase
  • Purchased more than 7 days ago

3. Eligible Refund Scenarios

We may consider refunds in the following limited circumstances:

  • Technical Issues: If a technical error resulted in duplicate charges or incorrect credit amounts
  • Service Unavailability: If the service was completely unavailable for an extended period (more than 48 hours) immediately after purchase
  • Unauthorized Charges: If you can demonstrate that a purchase was made without your authorization
  • Unused Credits: Credits that have not been used within 7 days of purchase may be eligible for a partial refund

4. Refund Request Process

To request a refund:

  • Contact our support team at support@cvapplyr.com within 7 days of purchase
  • Provide your transaction ID and order details
  • Explain the reason for your refund request
  • Include any supporting documentation (screenshots, error messages, etc.)

5. Refund Processing

If your refund request is approved:

  • Refunds will be processed within 5-10 business days
  • The refund will be issued to the original payment method
  • Any credits associated with the refunded purchase will be deducted from your account
  • You will receive a confirmation email once the refund is processed

6. Partial Refunds

In some cases, we may offer a partial refund based on:

  • The number of credits already used
  • The time elapsed since purchase
  • The specific circumstances of your case

7. Credit Expiration

Credits have a validity period as specified at the time of purchase. We do not offer refunds for expired credits. It is your responsibility to use credits before they expire.

8. Payment Disputes

If you dispute a charge with your payment provider before contacting us:

  • Your account may be suspended pending resolution
  • We may not be able to process a refund through our normal channels
  • Please contact us first to resolve any billing issues

9. Service Modifications

We reserve the right to modify our pricing and credit packages. However, changes will not affect credits already purchased unless we discontinue the service entirely.

10. Free Credits and Promotions

Free credits, promotional credits, or bonus credits are not eligible for refunds under any circumstances.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Refund Policy or wish to request a refund, please contact us at:

Email: support@cvapplyr.com

Response Time: Within 24-48 hours

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
