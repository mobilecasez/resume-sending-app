11. Google API Services

CVApplyr uses Google OAuth 2.0 to authenticate users and access Google services on your behalf. By signing in with Google, you grant us the following permissions:

11.1 Google Account Information

We access your Google Account email address and basic profile information (name, profile picture) to:

  • Create and authenticate your CVApplyr account
  • Personalize your experience within the app
  • Display your account information in the app interface

Scopes requested:

  • openid - Associate you with your personal info on Google
  • .../auth/userinfo.email - See your primary Google Account email address
  • .../auth/userinfo.profile - See your personal info, including any personal info you've made publicly available

11.2 Gmail API - Send Email

We request permission to send emails on your behalf using the Gmail API.

Scope requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send

Purpose: This permission is used solely to:

  • Send job application emails directly from your Gmail account
  • Attach your resume and cover letter to application emails
  • Ensure emails are sent from your personal email address (not a third-party server)

11.3 Gmail API - Read Email (Reply Checking)

We request permission to read your Gmail messages for a specific purpose.

Scope requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.metadata

Purpose: This permission is used only to:

  • Check for replies from companies you applied to
  • Match incoming emails with job applications you sent through CVApplyr
  • Notify you when employers respond to your applications

Important limitations:

  • We only search for unread emails from the last 30 days
  • We only check emails from company addresses you applied to
  • We do NOT read, store, or access any other emails in your inbox
  • We do NOT access emails sent to you by personal contacts
  • Email content is never stored on our servers

11.4 What We Do NOT Do

CVApplyr does NOT:

  • Read, access, or store personal emails unrelated to job applications
  • Delete or modify any emails in your Gmail account
  • Access your Gmail contacts or address book
  • Access draft emails or spam folder
  • Share your Gmail data with third parties or advertisers
  • Use your email data for marketing or analytics purposes

11.5 Data Storage and Security

Your Google OAuth tokens are:

  • Encrypted using industry-standard encryption (AES-256) and stored securely in our database
  • Used only to perform actions you explicitly request (sending applications, checking replies)
  • Never shared with third parties or external services
  • Automatically refreshed to maintain access without requiring you to re-authenticate
  • Deleted immediately when you disconnect your Google account or delete your CVApplyr account

11.6 Revoking Access

You can revoke CVApplyr's access to your Google Account at any time:

  • Visit Google Account Permissions
  • Find "CVApplyr" in the list of connected apps
  • Click "Remove Access" to immediately revoke all permissions
  • Or delete your CVApplyr account from the app settings

After revoking access, CVApplyr will no longer be able to send emails or check for replies on your behalf. Your stored OAuth tokens will be deleted from our servers.

11.7 Compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy

CVApplyr's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We:

  • Only request the minimum scopes necessary for our app's functionality
  • Use Gmail data solely for the features disclosed to users
  • Do not transfer Gmail data to third parties (except as necessary to provide the service)
  • Do not use Gmail data for serving advertisements
  • Do not allow humans to read Gmail data unless explicitly permitted by the user or required for security purposes
