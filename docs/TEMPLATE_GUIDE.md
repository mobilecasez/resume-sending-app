## What Gets Customized for Each Company?

### ✅ Automatically Updated:
- Company name ("Google" in example above)
- Position title ("Senior Software Engineer")
- Recipient name ("Hiring Manager")
- Date (always current date)
- Email subject line

### 🎯 You Customize Per Application:
- **Company-specific paragraph** (paragraph 2)
  - Why you're interested in THIS company
  - What excites you about their mission/products
  - How you align with their values
  
- **Relevant skills** (paragraph 3)
  - Skills most relevant to THIS job posting
  - Technologies they mention in the job description
  - Your strongest matching qualifications

## Examples of Good Company Paragraphs

### For Google:
```
I am particularly drawn to Google's mission to organize the world's 
information and make it universally accessible. Your commitment to 
innovation and creating products that impact billions of users aligns 
perfectly with my passion for building scalable solutions.
```

### For a Startup:
```
I am excited about [Company]'s innovative approach to disrupting the 
[industry] space. As someone who thrives in fast-paced environments 
and enjoys wearing multiple hats, I believe my entrepreneurial mindset 
and technical skills would be a great fit for your growing team.
```

### For Amazon:
```
Amazon's customer obsession and ownership principles resonate deeply 
with my approach to software development. I'm particularly drawn to 
the opportunity to work at scale and contribute to services that 
millions of customers depend on daily.
```

### For Microsoft:
```
Microsoft's transformation into a cloud-first company and commitment 
to empowering every person and organization on the planet aligns 
perfectly with my career goals. I'm especially excited about Azure's 
role in helping businesses innovate and scale.
```

## Tips for Writing Company Paragraphs

✅ **DO:**
- Research the company's mission/values
- Mention specific products or initiatives
- Show genuine interest and enthusiasm
- Keep it 2-3 sentences
- Be authentic

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use generic statements that could apply to any company
- Make it too long (keep under 100 words)
- Copy from their website verbatim
- Exaggerate or be dishonest

## Customizing Skills List

Match the job description! If they ask for:
- "Python, Django, PostgreSQL, AWS" → List those
- "React, TypeScript, Node.js, GraphQL" → List those
- "Java, Spring Boot, Microservices, Kubernetes" → List those

The skills get inserted into: 
> "My technical expertise includes [YOUR SKILLS], which align perfectly 
> with the requirements for this role."

**Remember:** The more customized your company paragraph and skills list, 
the better your application will stand out! 🌟
