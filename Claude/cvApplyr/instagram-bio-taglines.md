# cvApplyr — Instagram bio taglines

A bank of short, punchy taglines to use in your IG bio, profile header, or as opening lines in posts. Pick a favorite, mix & match, or rotate them to A/B test what resonates.

All translations are crafted (not Google-translated) — they read naturally in each language and keep the brand's confident, slightly-cheeky tone.

---

## The 4 English taglines

1. **Job hunting, but you're actually winning.** *(the brand tagline — confident, memorable)*
2. **Your career copilot.** *(short, brandable, ownable)*
3. **Apply less. Land more.** *(benefit-first, punchy)*
4. **Cover letters that mention the company by name.** *(differentiator-led)*

## Tagline rotation idea

Don't overthink the first version of the bio. Pick one of these for now and swap every 2-3 weeks. Track which one gets you more profile-visit-to-follow conversions (Instagram Insights shows this).

**Week 1-2:** "Your career copilot. Auto-applies + tracks replies."
**Week 3-4:** "Apply less. Land more. AI career copilot inside ↓"
**Week 5-6:** "Cover letters that mention the company by name. Free trial ↓"
**Week 7-8:** "Job hunting, but you're actually winning. 5 free credits ↓"

The bio you land on after 6-8 weeks of A/B testing will be 10× better than whatever you committed to on day one.
