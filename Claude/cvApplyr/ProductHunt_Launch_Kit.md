## 📖 LONG DESCRIPTION (for PH listing body)

**CVApplyr — Your AI Career Copilot**

Job hunting is broken. You spend hours tailoring cover letters, copying and pasting the same information, and then... silence. Most applicants hear nothing back.

We built CVApplyr to fix that.

**Here's what CVApplyr does:**

🤖 **AI Cover Letters in Seconds** — paste a job description, and CVApplyr generates a hyper-personalized cover letter tailored to that exact role and company. Not a generic template — a real letter that references the company's work.

📧 **Sends From Your Own Email** — letters go out from your Gmail or Outlook. No third-party email, no spam filters. It feels personal because it is.

⚡ **Bulk Application Mode** — use "Generate & Send All" to apply to multiple companies in one tap. One credit. Zero manual effort.

📊 **Application Tracking** — follow every application through Sent → Opened → Replied → Interview. Know exactly where you stand.

🎯 **Real Results** — our users see up to 33% reply rates vs. the industry average of under 5%.

🔥 **Streak & Credits System** — stay motivated with daily streaks and gamified progress.

**Who is this for?**
- Professionals actively job hunting
- Recent graduates sending bulk applications
- Career changers who need to reframe their experience fast
- Anyone tired of spending 2 hours per application

**Free to get started.** Premium plans for power users.

We're a small team that built this after personally experiencing the frustration of job hunting. We'd love your feedback — every upvote and comment helps us keep building.

👉 Try it free: [cvapplyr.com](https://cvapplyr.com)
