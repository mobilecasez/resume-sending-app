# CVApplyr — Telegram Community Strategy

---

## 🎯 Goal

Get in front of active job seekers in Telegram groups. Offer genuine value, collect feedback, and convert engaged members into early users.

---

## 🔍 Where to Find the Right Groups

Search these keywords inside Telegram's search bar:

| Search Term | What You'll Find |
|---|---|
| `jobs hiring` | General job boards |
| `software jobs` | Tech job seekers |
| `IT jobs India` | Indian tech market |
| `placement 2025` / `placement 2026` | Fresh graduates |
| `remote work` | Remote job hunters |
| `job seekers` | Active hunters |
| `startup jobs` | Startup-focused |
| `LinkedIn jobs` | People who take job search seriously |
| `H1B jobs` | International job seekers in the US |
| `Canada jobs` | Expat job market |

**Also join:**
- Developer/freelancer communities (Indie Hackers Telegram, etc.)
- Career coaching groups
- Country-specific expat job groups

---

## 📋 Rules Before Posting

1. **Join and observe for 24–48 hours** — understand the tone and rules
2. **Read pinned messages** — many groups ban promotional posts
3. **Engage first** — reply to 2–3 other threads before posting yours
4. **Never use the word "advertisement"** or make it feel commercial
5. **Offer something real** — free credits, personal help, feedback session

## 💡 Tips for High-Conversion Telegram Posts

- **Use emojis** — they break up text and increase readability in dark-mode chat
- **Keep it under 150 words** — long messages get scrolled past
- **End with a clear action** — link, DM, or question
- **Post between 7–10 PM local time** — that's when most people check Telegram
- **Pin your message** — if you admin a group, pin your CVApplyr intro post

## 🏗️ Create Your Own Telegram Channel

Once you have 50+ users, create an official CVApplyr channel:

**Channel name:** CVApplyr — Job Hunting Tips
**What to post:**
- Daily job search tips (30-second reads)
- Feature updates
- User success stories ("got an interview at X using CVApplyr!")
- Occasional polls and questions

This builds a direct audience you own — no algorithm, no ads.
