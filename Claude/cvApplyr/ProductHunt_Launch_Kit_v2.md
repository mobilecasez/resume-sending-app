## 3. Long description

**Most AI job tools compete on how much they can do without you. CVApplyr competes on knowing when to stop.**

Here's the whole trip. You search with the real Google, in a full web view inside the app, with your own session. Not a scraped card list that went stale last month: any careers page, any country, any language. Found a role? One tap saves it. The AI reads the actual posting, even on sites it has never seen, and captures the hiring email when the posting names one.

Then it helps you apply where the job actually lives: the company's own application form. Auto Fill completes Greenhouse, Workday, Personio, Ashby and plenty of others. Dropdowns, comboboxes, dial codes, multi-step wizards, shadow DOM. It attaches your resume and a cover letter written from the posting it just read. (This is why the browser lives inside the app, by the way. Try filling a Workday wizard in mobile Safari.)

Then it stops.

### The line we drew

- **It never hits submit.** You review every application, and nothing goes anywhere without your finger on the button.
- **It never answers visa, work-authorization, salary, or demographic questions.** Those land on a "Still needs you" list, on purpose. They're consequential, and they're yours.
- **Email applications go out from your own Gmail or Outlook.** Not from a bot, not from an address you've never seen. Replies come back to you.

That's not a missing feature. It's the feature.

### Built for crossing borders

- **Country-correct documents.** Upload a resume or paste your messy career story into the AI builder, then pick the format the country expects: German Lebenslauf-style with photo, UK CV, US resume. PDF or Word.
- **Cover letters in the country's conventions,** written from the captured posting, not from a blob you pasted. Preview the letter free before you spend a credit.
- **Translate any job page to English, in place.** The layout stays, the words change, and it works even on portals where the usual translation widgets are blocked. Built for reading German, Dutch, and French postings.

If you're applying into a country that isn't your own, the visa question is your answer to give, not an AI's guess.

### The rest of the loop

Where a posting names a hiring contact, one tap opens a ready-to-send email with your documents attached, and reply notifications mean you see the response. The app can also find recruiter and HR contacts — with verified emails where available. When an application form actually submits, the job is marked Applied automatically, and everything lives in one My Jobs dashboard with match % scoring against your resume, saved jobs, and a letters library.

### Pricing, plainly

Free to download, credit packs for AI actions. Browsing, searching, and tracking cost nothing; credits pay for the AI work, and cover-letter previews are free, so you see the letter before you spend anything.

Every application that leaves CVApplyr was read, finished, and sent by a human: you.

iOS, Android, and web at cvapplyr.com.

## 11. Prepared answers to the three likely objections (public-safe)

**"How is this different from the auto-apply bots?"**
Opposite philosophy. Those tools submit for you, and they'll guess at visa, salary, and demographic questions on your behalf. CVApplyr never auto-submits and deliberately refuses to answer those questions. It fills everything it safely can on the company's own form, shows you a "Still needs you" list, and you review before submitting. The letter it attaches is written from the actual posting it just read, not a template. Fewer applications, but every one finished by a human. We automate the typing, not the judgment.

**"Autofill always breaks. Does it really work on the big ATS platforms?"**
Form-filling is genuinely hard and I won't claim 100%. It handles the major platforms (Greenhouse, Workday, Personio, Ashby and others), including dropdowns, comboboxes, phone dial codes, multi-step wizards, and shadow-DOM widgets, and it works on forms we've never seen because it reads the page rather than running a per-site script. When it can't fill something, it fails safe: the field lands on the "Still needs you" list instead of getting a wrong guess, and nothing submits without you. If a specific site breaks, flag it in the app and we fix employers individually. No promised timelines, though. Honest answer.

**"Why credits instead of a subscription?"**
Because job searching is bursty. The app is free to download, and browsing, searching, and tracking cost nothing. Credits pay for the actions that cost real AI money: reading a posting, writing a letter, filling a form. A casual month costs you nothing and a heavy week costs a few dollars, instead of a monthly subscription you forget to cancel after you're hired. Packs run $4.99 (20 credits) to $49.99 (500 credits), the same price on every platform — and cover-letter previews are free, so you see the letter before you spend anything.

# INTERNAL APPENDIX — COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE
**INTERNAL — DO NOT PASTE anywhere public. Brand names are allowed here and nowhere else.**

A PH reader slots "AI job application app" into one of six boxes within seconds. The launch copy's first job is breaking the worst slotting (mass auto-applier). Reference before replying to comments.

**1. Mass auto-appliers — LazyApply, Sonara, AIApply, JobRight (auto-apply mode).** They genuinely deliver volume and "applied while you slept" dashboards. Known weaknesses: templated spray recruiters can smell, auto-answering visa/salary/demographic questions (sometimes wrongly), silent breakage on real forms. Our true gap: never auto-submits, "Still needs you" list, letters from the actual captured posting. Comment line: "we automate the typing, not the judgment." Never cite reply rates (the 33% stat is founder/test data and banned). Don't name LazyApply even if a commenter does; say "mass auto-appliers."

**2. Trackers / copilots — Teal, Huntr, Simplify, Careerflow.** Genuinely good Kanban tracking; Simplify's extension is the closest desktop analog to Auto Fill; strong free tiers and communities. Weaknesses: desktop/extension-first, manual "applied" state, English/US assumptions. Our gap: the whole loop natively on the phone, automatic Applied detection on real form submits, cross-border documents and in-place translation.

**3. Resume / ATS tools — Jobscan, Rezi, Kickresume, Resume Worded.** Jobscan's match reports are the standard; Rezi/Kickresume make good ATS-safe documents fast. Weaknesses: they stop at the document, US conventions, scoring against pasted JDs. Our gap: documents that travel into the actual application (auto-attach on Auto Fill and email apply), country formats incl. Lebenslauf with photo, match % against jobs you actually saved.

**4. Boards / aggregators — Indeed, Welcome to the Jungle (Otta), Hiring Cafe, JobRight discovery, StepStone and regional boards.** Unmatched inventory; Hiring Cafe's direct-from-ATS index is beloved by exactly the PH crowd, so expect the comparison in comments. Weaknesses: walled gardens, stale copies, ghost jobs, cross-border blind spots. Our gap: we don't compete on inventory; the search tab is the real Google with the user's own session. If Hiring Cafe comes up: they index jobs, we're the apply layer on the open web; complimentary tone, no disparagement.

**5. LinkedIn (Easy Apply + Premium).** The default: identity, network, one-tap apply, Premium at $30-40/month. Weaknesses: Easy Apply floods (thousands of applicants because applying is free-effort; many recruiters weight direct-ATS applications higher), large parts of European SME/government/non-English hiring aren't there, subscription pricing. Our gap: the applications that aren't one tap (Workday/Personio/Greenhouse forms, unreadable postings, "email your CV to…" via the user's own Gmail/Outlook). Positioning: complementary in reality; "LinkedIn is where you get seen; CVApplyr is how you actually apply everywhere else."

**6. Contact tools — Hunter.io, Apollo, RocketReach.** Deep verified B2B databases priced for sales teams. Our gap: built in, sized for a job seeker who needs three emails a week, attached to the job just saved, flowing into apply-via-email. In replies always use the exact phrase: "find recruiter and HR contacts — with verified emails where available." Remember: verified work-email discovery is web-only today; mobile shows Coming Soon.

**One-line internal positioning summary.** Against auto-appliers: we keep the human in the loop. Against trackers: mobile-native, and we close the loop automatically. Against resume tools: our documents actually travel into the application. Against boards and LinkedIn: the whole internet is our job board, in any language.

Credit costs are admin-configurable server-side; verify live values before quoting them anywhere.
