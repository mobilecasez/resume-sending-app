## POST 3 — Insight / Thought Leadership (Builds Authority)

---

Hiring managers spend 7 seconds on a cover letter.

Here's what makes them actually read it:

1. **Line 1 is specific** — not "I'm excited to apply." Something like: "I noticed your team just launched [product] — my last 3 years were building the exact same thing at [company]."

2. **One number in the first paragraph** — any number. Impact is concrete. "I grew X by Y%" beats "I have extensive experience in X."

3. **It mentions the company, not just the role** — shows research. Anyone can send the same letter to 100 companies. Showing you read their blog or know their product signals genuine interest.

4. **Short** — 3 paragraphs max. Hiring managers don't read cover letters. They skim them. A long letter is a guaranteed skip.

5. **Strong close** — don't end with "I look forward to hearing from you." End with confidence: "I'm confident I can contribute meaningfully to [specific team/goal] and would love to talk."

I built CVApplyr to automate exactly this structure — because writing it manually for every application is exhausting.

But even if you never use the tool, these 5 things will dramatically improve your reply rate.
